# Changelog

## [v1.0.0]

First release of Wave.

- YouTube Music backend: search, home, charts, artists, albums, radio, playback
- Sign in with Google: YouTube Music playlists and likes sync automatically
- Sign in with Spotify: playlists and Liked Songs sync automatically
- Spotify tracks play their YouTube Music equivalent
- Playlist import from YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and Amazon Music links,
  text and CSV
- Synced lyrics from LRCLIB
- UI from AlgerMusicPlayer; NetEase backend removed
